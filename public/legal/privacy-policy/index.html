Seller's Filter Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 18, 2026
Last Updated: June 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Seller's Filter ("Seller's Filter," "we," "our," or "us") is operated by Desert Brick Software LLC.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, and the choices available to users of the Seller's Filter service.

Seller's Filter is designed around a data-minimization philosophy. We intentionally collect and retain only the information reasonably necessary to operate listings and buyer inquiries.

By using Seller's Filter, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. About Seller's Filter

Seller's Filter is a communication and information-sharing platform that helps marketplace sellers organize buyer inquiries through structured listing pages.

Seller's Filter is not a marketplace, payment processor, escrow service, shipping provider, broker, or marketplace facilitator.

Seller's Filter does not process transactions or payments between users.

4. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate and maintain Seller's Filter
  • Create and display listings
  • Deliver buyer inquiries to sellers
  • Respond to support requests
  • Improve service reliability and performance
  • Prevent abuse, spam, fraud, and misuse
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect the security of the service

We do not use information to build advertising profiles or behavioral marketing profiles.

5. Buyer Inquiry Message Handling

Seller's Filter is designed to minimize retention of buyer inquiry content.

When a buyer submits an inquiry:

  • The message is transmitted through Seller's Filter infrastructure.
  • The message is delivered to the seller using our email delivery provider.
  • Seller's Filter does not intentionally maintain an archive of buyer message content after successful transmission.

However, we may retain limited operational metadata related to message processing, including:

  • Listing identifiers
  • Timestamps
  • Message length
  • Delivery status
  • Security and abuse-prevention information
  • Diagnostic information

This metadata does not include retained copies of buyer message bodies.

Temporary processing, transient system caching, backups, error logging, email delivery systems, and similar operational processes may result in short-term retention of information outside of Seller's Filter's intended retention practices. Seller's Filter does not intentionally maintain a long-term archive of buyer message content after successful delivery.

6. Email Communications

Seller email addresses are not publicly displayed on listing pages.

Seller's Filter may use seller and buyer email addresses solely for purposes related to:

  • Listing management
  • Inquiry delivery
  • Service communications
  • Operational notifications

We do not sell email addresses or use them for third-party marketing purposes.

7. Images and Uploaded Content

Sellers may upload images associated with listings.

By uploading images, users acknowledge that:

  • Images may become publicly visible on listing pages.
  • Images may contain metadata, including location information or other embedded data.
  • Seller's Filter does not guarantee removal of image metadata.
  • Users are responsible for reviewing uploaded images before submission.

Users are solely responsible for the content they upload.

8. Edit Tokens and Listing Access

Seller's Filter currently does not use traditional user accounts.

Listing management functionality is controlled through unique edit links or edit tokens.

Possession of a valid edit token may allow modification of the associated listing.

Users are responsible for safeguarding edit links and edit tokens.

9. Data Retention

Seller's Filter seeks to retain information only as long as reasonably necessary.

Listing information may be retained while a listing remains active.

Images associated with sold, deleted, removed, or inactive listings may be deleted from storage when they are no longer reasonably necessary for service operation. Deletion may occur through manual or automated processes and may not occur immediately upon a listing status change.

Operational logs and metadata may be retained for:

  • Security
  • Abuse prevention
  • Diagnostics
  • Service administration
  • Legal compliance

Retention periods may vary depending on operational needs and legal requirements.
