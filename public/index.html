Organize buyer inquiries. Skip the chaos.

A simpler way to share item details and handle buyer messages

PickupDetails helps marketplace sellers create a simple listing page and collect better buyer inquiries without repeatedly answering the same questions in DMs.

Create a Listing

Who it's for

People selling items through Facebook Marketplace, OfferUp, Craigslist, local groups, or similar platforms who want a cleaner way to share details and manage buyer interest.

Why use PickupDetails

The goal is better buyer interactions, fewer repetitive messages, and a cleaner way to share item details — so you spend less time in back-and-forth DMs and more time on the sale.

How it works

  1. Create your listing Add your item details and photos to build a shareable PickupDetails page.
  2. Share your PickupDetails link Send the link to interested buyers instead of retyping the same info in every message.
  3. Receive organized buyer inquiries PickupDetails is being built to send structured buyer inquiries to your email, so common questions are answered up front.

PickupDetails is currently in early testing. Features may change as the product improves.
