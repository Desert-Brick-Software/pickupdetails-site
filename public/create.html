How to get the best results

Buyer inquiries from your Seller's Filter page will be sent to the email address you used when creating your listing. Be sure to keep an eye on your inbox (and your spam folder the first time) so you don't miss interested buyers.

We recommend prioritizing the inquiries you receive through your Seller's Filter page whenever possible. Those buyers have already taken a little extra time to review your listing before reaching out, helping you identify people who have shown a higher level of interest than a quick one-tap message.

You may still receive messages through your chosen marketplace. Seller's Filter won't eliminate low-effort messages, but it can make it easier to identify buyers who are genuinely interested.
